# SEVEN

> **Play it: https://pappydapimp69.github.io/Seven/**

Seven of you set out to walk a trail through the woods and come out the other
side. That is what the game tells you, right to the end. What it is about is who
walks out with you.

- Seven stretches of woods, south to north. A fallen trunk seals each one;
  cutting it takes hours, and hours are daylight.
- At camp, at dawn and at dusk, you set everyone's work. Nowhere else.
- The fire keeps the camp safe at night — the camp only. The woodpile stays
  where you leave it.
- Pylons take two pairs of hands and hold off the deep woods for a day and a half.
- Some nights someone walks off into the trees, and something with their name
  takes their place. Nobody finds it strange. At first light, a voice calls.
- Ask anyone about a day you both lived through. A real account is the day as it
  happened. A false one has exactly one thing wrong, derived from what happened.
- At the far side there is one more pylon than you have real people.

Controls: WASD/arrows to walk · E act · F fire · Q camp · R rest · C whistle ·
Esc pause. Touch and gamepad work too.
